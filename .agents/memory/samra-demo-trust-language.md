---
name: Samra demo trust language
description: Product and disclosure conventions for the frontend-only Samra Pay concept site.
---

All homepage metrics, rates, card benefits, and application CTAs must be framed as illustrative demo content until live financial services exist. Primary actions should route to a functioning demo or quote flow, and privacy, terms, and disclosure affordances should be real navigable links.

**Why:** Samra Pay is currently a client-side product demonstration. Clear boundaries prevent visitors from mistaking mock balances, promotional rates, card benefits, or sign-in flows for live financial offers.

**How to apply:** When adding or revising public-facing financial copy, label illustrative figures near the claim, avoid unsupported guarantees, and ensure the CTA matches the actual destination and behavior.
