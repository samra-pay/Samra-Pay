## Go/no-go decision

**GO—with corrected scope and sequencing.**

This is the right work to complete before broader mobile API integration. A governed design system will reduce visual and accessibility drift when mobile moves from mock data to backend truth.

**NO-GO** on Replit’s original recommendation as written because:

- `tokens.json` and its generator already exist.
- The work should not run as loosely coordinated parallel changes.
- The mobile app, backend, deployment, and database must remain untouched.
- Replit must not claim S-tier without automated and documented acceptance gates.
- The resulting ZIP must exclude `dist`, `.tsbuildinfo`, `.replit-artifact`, credentials, and dependency caches.
