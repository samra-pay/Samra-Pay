# Approved Samra Pay card artwork v1.0

Status: **Approved brand artwork**. Decision owner: **David Haile**.
Approval date: **2026-09-09**.

David approved the four refined Samra Pay card fronts and requested that they
be locked as official designs and applied to the website. This supersedes the
earlier Samra One card studies and the public website's prior HTML/CSS card art.

The [approved Figma board](https://www.figma.com/design/XS05bi0uuU8YA1SoYu361Y?node-id=58-3)
is version 1.0. Its four native masters and the presentation/wallet copies are
locked against accidental edits. Future visual changes require a new version;
do not overwrite the approved source exports.

| Card | Finish | Native Figma master | Source export |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Samra Pay Charge | Graphite satin, silver etched detail | `61:2` | `samra-pay-charge-v1.png` |
| Samra Pay Elite | Forest satin, warm brass accents | `63:2` | `samra-pay-elite-v1.png` |
| Samra Pay Airline | Champagne gold, supplied Ethiopian Airlines mark | `63:14` | `samra-pay-airline-v1.png` |
| Samra Pay Elite 100 | Silver titanium, founding member sample 001 / 100 | `62:2` | `samra-pay-elite-100-v1.png` |

Source exports live in
`artifacts/samra-pay/src/assets/coming-soon/source/`. They are 856 by 540 pixels.
The [approval manifest](../../artifacts/samra-pay/creative/cards/approved-v1.json)
records their SHA-256 hashes and provenance. The existing public-image pipeline
produces responsive AVIF/WebP derivatives for the website. Rendering the complete
front preserves the approved material, wordmark, etched border, chip, and marks.

The approved identity is **Samra Pay**, using bold Outfit for `samra` and italic
EB Garamond for `pay`. The sample founding number is illustrative, not an
assigned membership number.

Approval covers visual brand artwork and its requested website use. It does not
establish an issued card, manufacturing specifications, network authorization,
or an executed airline agreement. Existing product and co-brand disclosures
remain in place. Website source, verification, and Hosting publication are
recorded separately in the change/release evidence.
